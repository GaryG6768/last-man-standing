HOW IT WORKS

Last Man Standing Rules

  • 1. The game
    Last Man Standing is a football prediction game for fun. Each week every entrant selects one Premier League team to win its match.
  • 2. Going through
    If your selected team wins, you go through to the next round. If your selected team draws or loses, you are eliminated.
  • 3. Entry fee
    The standard entry fee is £5 per player. Entry fees are paid to the organiser. The app does not process payments.
  • 4. Normal weekly deadline
    For a normal weekend round, selections must be made by 18:00 on Friday.
  • 5. Midweek rounds
    For a midweek round, the deadline is 18:00 on the day the round starts. This rule takes priority over the normal Friday deadline. If a round covers several days, the deadline remains 18:00 on the first day of the round.
  • 6. Missed selection
    If no selection is made by the deadline, the player will receive the next available team from the bottom of the league upwards, starting with the lowest-ranked team they have not previously used, provided that team is actually playing in that round. The first missed selection results in a warning. A second missed selection results in elimination.
  • 7. Same team cannot be used twice
    A team can only be selected once during the competition. This applies to normal selections, replacement selections and automatic selections.
  • 8. Changing your selection
    Your selection can be changed any number of times before the published deadline. At 18:00 the selection is locked and cannot normally be changed. The final selection at the deadline is the one that counts.
  • 9. Published selections
    Once selections are locked, the selections are published so that everyone can see the full list before the new game starts.
  • 10. Postponed fixtures
    If your selected fixture is postponed after the round has started, you go through that round, but the selected team remains unavailable for future selections.

    If the fixture is postponed more than two hours before kick-off, you must select a replacement team. The replacement can be selected until the replacement team's kick-off.

    If the postponement occurs less than two hours before kick-off, the fixture is treated as abandoned under the abandoned-game rules.
  • 11. Abandoned fixtures
    An abandoned selected game follows the postponed-game rules. Other results in the round normally stand. However, if the Last Man Standing competition would be won because of the postponed or abandoned game, all games in that round are abandoned and nobody can be declared the winner for that round.
  • 12. Competition cannot be won by a postponed or abandoned game
    A Last Man Standing competition cannot be declared won because of a postponed or abandoned fixture.
  • 13. Postponement before the round starts
    If a selected fixture is postponed before the round begins, the player must select another team.

    If the postponement occurs after the 18:00 deadline but before the round starts, the replacement team can be selected until that replacement team's kick-off. The replacement team cannot have been used previously.
  • 14. Rollover
    The competition must be won by an individual player. If there is no individual winner, the prize pot rolls over.

    Existing participants who re-enter pay £5. New players pay an increased entry fee according to the rollover number.

    On rollover 1: existing players £5, new players £10.

    On rollover 2: existing players £5, new players £15.

    On rollover 3: existing players £5, new players £20.

    The existing prize pot is preserved.
  • 15. Players who do not re-enter
    A player who does not re-enter a rollover has no claim to any money in the prize pot.
  • 16. End of Premier League season
    If the competition is still running when the Premier League season ends, the competition pauses and restarts with the surviving players in the following season.

    This is not a rollover. Previous team selections remain unavailable and the players do not reset their used teams or pay an additional £5 simply because the season has ended.
  • 17. Payment
    All fees must normally be paid before the relevant game commences unless the organiser has agreed otherwise. Players whose payment is not received can be removed from the competition.

    The organiser can mark a player as paid, payment due, waived/approved or removed.
